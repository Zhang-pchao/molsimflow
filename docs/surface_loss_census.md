# Prospective count-loss census

`prospective_count_losses` registers all initial states whose count and past history satisfy caller-defined criteria. It reports the first observed net decrease and available follow-up independently. Gaps, source boundaries and trajectory ends censor windows. A shortened window with no observed loss is not a full-window negative. Oxygen identities, species, adsorption, connected geometry and event independence remain caller responsibilities; this utility alone never identifies water evacuation.

Counts can remain constant during complete identity turnover. Fractional loss thresholds use the initial population, and all candidates are kept without selecting their later refill or occupation outcomes.
