# Qualified radial excursions

Reuse existing core-to-core excursions inside strictly contiguous valid site
records. Near requires qualified water oxygen within the inner radius. Clear
requires all solution-origin oxygen beyond the outer radius, including oxygen
that does not currently qualify as water. Their union covers all solution O;
a qualification change alone therefore cannot create clearance.

A near-to-clear pair establishes a lower bound on the relative radial change
of the oxygen identified at the near endpoint: its near distance is known and
its far distance is at least the nearest-any-solution-O distance at the clear
endpoint. Endpoint nearest-water identity changes alone do not establish
molecule exchange. Directions, intervening-water geometry and causal roles
require additional evidence.

Return and censored excursions are not automatically small fluctuations.
Use an explicit union mask when weighting hit intervals to avoid counting
shared endpoints twice. These are saved-frame geometric observations, not
independent rate events, continuous paths or metastable states.
